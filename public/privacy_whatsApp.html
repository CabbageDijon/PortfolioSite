WhatsApp Tool Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our WhatsApp tool.

Overview

This privacy policy supplements the main CabsCode Privacy Policy and specifically covers the data processed by our WhatsApp interactive business tool.

What We Collect via WhatsApp

When you use our WhatsApp tool, we collect and store only what is necessary to deliver your requested service:

  • Business Name — to brand your documents.
  • Logo Image — uploaded or sent in chat to render on your PDFs.
  • Brand Color — your preferred accent color for generated documents.
  • Contact Details — your phone number and any business contact info you provide.
  • Invoice/Document Items — descriptions, quantities, prices, and client details you send for the invoices or receipts you wish to generate.

Purpose of Data Collection

The primary purpose of collecting this information is to generate PDFs (such as professional invoices and receipts) and send them back to you directly on WhatsApp.

Data Storage & Trials

We do not share, sell, or rent your data to any third parties. We store your information solely for the following operational purposes:

  • Tracking your trial count (up to 5 free documents).
  • Managing active subscriptions and verification for paid plans.

Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. If you wish to delete all stored information associated with your WhatsApp number and business profile, please email us directly at:
cabscodepro@gmail.com
Upon receiving your request, all corresponding records, logos, and trial counts will be permanently removed.

General Terms

For information regarding website cookies, hosting logs, and general site rights under the Botswana Data Protection Act, please review the main CabsCode Privacy Policy.
