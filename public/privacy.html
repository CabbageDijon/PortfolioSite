Privacy Policy

What personal data this site collects, why, and your rights under the Botswana Data Protection Act.

Who We Are

CabsCode (cabscode.pro) is operated by Tema Bown in Gaborone, Botswana. Contact: services@cabscode.pro.

Data We Collect

  • Project inquiries — email, phone, selected service, message and quote details.
  • SEO audits — business name, type, location, URL and search terms. Email verification codes are hashed and expire in 15 minutes.
  • Preferences — dark/light theme choice, stored in your own browser and never sent to us.

This site sets no tracking cookies of its own. Hosting infrastructure may process standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver pages securely and limit abuse.

How We Use It

To respond to your project requests, deliver SEO audit results, and prevent spam. Your data is never sold or rented.

Third Parties

Trusted providers process data strictly to run site features: email delivery, search and AI APIs for SEO audits, and hosting.

Links that take you to external services (such as LinkedIn, GitHub and live demo sites) are outside CabsCode's control — their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Retention

Verification codes expire in 15 minutes. Weekly audit limits use anonymised email hashes. Inquiry emails are kept until you ask for deletion.

Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act of Botswana you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing services@cabscode.pro.

Security

Forms are rate-limited and spam-checked. Avoid sending passwords, ID numbers or banking details through the forms.
