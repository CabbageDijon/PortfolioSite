#17 & #18 — Deduplication recommendations

  • Nav & footer: The nav bar (~75 lines) and footer (~45 lines) are copy-pasted across all 10+ HTML files. Any change requires editing every page. Suggested fix: add a minimal build step (SSI, Node script, or static-site generator).
  • Logo SVG: The 32-line inline SVG is repeated verbatim in every page. Suggested fix: extract to a standalone images/logo.svg and reference via <img src="images/logo.svg"> — also makes it cacheable.
